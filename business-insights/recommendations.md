# Business Recommendations

## 1. Introduce margin-based discount controls

Use minimum-margin thresholds for promotional campaigns. The observed margin decline from 35.40% at no discount to 11.28% at 21–30% discounts indicates that broad high-discount campaigns should be reviewed carefully.

## 2. Prioritize customer retention and reactivation

Use RFM segments to differentiate customer actions. Champions and Loyal Customers can receive loyalty-oriented benefits, while At Risk and Lost Customers can be targeted with carefully designed reactivation campaigns.

## 3. Investigate delivery bottlenecks

A 40% late-delivery rate among delivered orders warrants operational investigation. Review carrier performance, fulfillment locations, service-level agreements and the causes of late orders by shipping mode.

## 4. Reduce preventable returns

Quality issues and damaged products together account for 49% of returns. Quality-control checks, packaging improvements and product-level defect monitoring should be prioritized.

## 5. Protect category and product profitability

Revenue should not be evaluated without profit and margin. Product-level monitoring should flag items with weak or negative profit and trigger price, cost, sourcing or promotion reviews.

## 6. Build a recurring management dashboard

The Power BI dashboard can be reused as a monthly management view with KPIs covering revenue, profit, customer retention, RFM segments, delivery performance and returns.
