# Executive Summary

## Project

E-Commerce Customer & Sales Intelligence

## Scope

The analysis covers 12,000 orders placed during 2025 across 3,921 purchasing customers, 300 products and five product categories. SQL was used for structured analysis, Python was used for exploratory analysis and visualization, and Power BI was used to build the interactive dashboard.

## Important Data Note

The earlier project draft contained a visual statement that the top 20% of customers contributed approximately 70% of revenue. Recalculation on the final 3,921-customer dataset shows that the top 20% contributed approximately 57.12% of revenue. The final GitHub README and portfolio material should use 57.12%, not the earlier estimate.
